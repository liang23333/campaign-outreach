# Master Canvassing & Door-Knocking Script

## 🚪 Step 1: Introduction (15–20 Seconds)
"Hi there! My name is [Volunteer Name], and I'm a volunteer neighbor with [Candidate Name]'s City Council campaign. We aren't here asking for money today — we're canvassing the neighborhood because [Candidate Name] is hosting an open Community Listening Tour across the East Bay to hear directly from residents about what needs to change in our city. Do you have two minutes to share what issues matter most to you and your family right now?"

---

## 👂 Step 2: Open-Ended Listening Question
"When you look at your neighborhood here in [Oakland / Berkeley / Richmond], what is one thing that you feel the City Council needs to tackle immediately?"

### Key Issue Prompts & Transitions
- **Affordable Housing & Rent:**  
  *Response:* "That resonates with so many neighbors we talk to. [Candidate] believes housing is a human right and is fighting to protect tenants from unfair evictions and build deeply affordable social housing. We’re discussing this in-depth at our listening session."
- **Public Transit & Safe Streets:**  
  *Response:* "Reliable, safe streets and frequent transit are essential for our daily lives. [Candidate] is prioritizing protected crosswalks, fixing chronic road potholes, and boosting bus reliability."
- **Environmental Justice & Public Health:**  
  *Response:* "Every community deserves clean air and safe parks. [Candidate] is holding corporate polluters accountable and fighting for community-controlled air monitoring and public health clinics."

---

## 🎟️ Step 3: Invitation to the Listening Tour
"We want to make sure your voice is part of the campaign's 100-Day Action Plan. [Candidate] is hosting our [City] Community Listening Town Hall on:
- **Date & Time:** [Date & Time for this stop]
- **Location:** [Venue Name & Address]

Can we count on you to join us or can I send you a reminder text?"

---

## 📝 Step 4: Closing & Casework Check
"Before I let you go, is there any urgent neighborhood issue (like a broken streetlamp, illegal dumping, or traffic signal issue) that you'd like us to submit to the city casework pipeline?"
- Record details on the canvasser sheet.
- Hand them the flyer.
- "Thank you so much for your time and for helping shape our campaign!"
