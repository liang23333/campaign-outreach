# Volunteer Guide & Day-of-Event Operations

Welcome to our campaign volunteer crew! Whether you're canvassing, flyering, or staffing an event, this guide provides all key details.

---

## 👥 Key Volunteer Roles

### 1. Neighborhood Captains
- Coordinate local flyering and canvassing shifts.
- Serve as the local point-of-contact for neighborhood groups and block associations.

### 2. Event Greeting & Registration Team
- Staff the welcome table, check in attendees, and distribute agenda packets.
- Manage sign-up sheets for voter registration and volunteer recruitment.

### 3. Note-Takers & Facilitation Assistants
- Sit in breakout groups during listening sessions to accurately record resident input.
- Transcribe action items and constituent casework into the campaign tracker.

### 4. Accessibility & Logistics Leads
- Ensure ADA ramps, microphone amplification, and audio setups are functioning.
- Support translation/interpretation headsets and child-friendly activities.

---

## ⏰ Day-of Schedule Template

- **T - 60 min:** Volunteer briefing, room setup, A/V testing, signage placement.
- **T - 15 min:** Doors open, sign-in begins, welcome music, refreshments available.
- **T + 00 min:** Welcome remarks by Neighborhood Captain & Candidate overview (15 min).
- **T + 15 min:** Open Community Listening Mic & breakout table discussions (75 min).
- **T + 90 min:** Candidate recap, immediate commitments, and next steps (20 min).
- **T + 110 min:** Informal networking, constituent casework intake, and volunteer sign-up (20 min).
- **T + 130 min:** Room teardown and debrief session.

---

## 📋 Code of Conduct
- **Respect & Inclusivity:** We welcome all residents regardless of political background. Listen with empathy.
- **Non-Partisan Voter Assistance:** We assist every eligible resident with voter registration in accordance with California law.
- **Safety First:** Canvass in pairs, stay hydrated, and report any health or safety concerns to the field director immediately.
